Vanta 1.6.0: a home dashboard, modpacks and world backups

PLAY opens with a dashboard, Modrinth modpacks install in one click with every file hash-checked, and worlds get one-click backups. Plus a crash helper, appearance settings and a native Mac window.

Vanta 1.6.0 is the biggest update so far, for Windows and macOS.

Home dashboard

PLAY now opens with Jump back in (your recent instances with their mods, worlds and when you last played), how many friends are online on your network, and what's new. It works offline.

Modpacks

INSTANCES › Modpacks searches Fabric modpacks on Modrinth, or imports a .mrpack file. Every file is checked against the pack's SHA-512 hash before anything changes. The instance's old mods and config are moved to a backup folder first, and the main 1.21.11 instance is never replaced. You can also export your own setup as a .mrpack.

Worlds and backups

See your worlds with their icons, back one up in a click, restore without overwriting, and delete only after an automatic backup.

And more

  • A crash helper that names the mods that were likely involved and can turn one off in one click.
  • Update all mods, Suggest RAM, and downloads that retry and never keep a file that fails its hash check.
  • Six purple-family accents, text size, high contrast and reduce motion. Ctrl+K quick jump.
  • On Mac, Vanta opens in its own native window, never your browser.
