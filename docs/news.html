News

One post per release, in plain words. For every detail and file hash, see the changelog. Subscribe with the RSS feed.

18 October 2026 · v1.6.6

Vanta 1.6.6: check mods from the crash card

After a crash, one button checks the mods of the instance that crashed. Turned-off mods can be turned back on, Home shows playtime this week, and the crash helper knows four more crashes.

18 October 2026 · v1.6.5

Vanta 1.6.5: one-click fixes for mod problems

The mod check can now fix the common problems for you, runs before joining servers too, and the crash card links to a searchable list of fixes.

18 October 2026 · v1.6.4

Vanta 1.6.4: a mod check before you play, playtime and notes

Vanta now spots broken mod setups before Minecraft starts, counts your playtime, keeps notes per instance and lets you browse mods offline.

18 October 2026 · v1.6.3

Vanta 1.6.3: copy your mods to another version

Set up a new Minecraft version in one click: Vanta adds your mods, shaders and packs to another instance, each downloaded for that version.

18 October 2026 · v1.6.2

Vanta 1.6.2: a downloads panel, instance names and cape previews

Pause, resume or cancel Minecraft downloads from one panel, give your instances their own names, and try capes and elytra on the 3D skin.

17 October 2026 · v1.6.1

Vanta 1.6.1: server favourites, a logs viewer and shader previews

Star your favourite servers, search the game log, flip through screenshots full size, and see shader and resource pack previews before you install.

17 October 2026 · v1.6.0

Vanta 1.6.0: a home dashboard, modpacks and world backups

PLAY opens with a dashboard, Modrinth modpacks install in one click with every file hash-checked, and worlds get one-click backups. Plus a crash helper, appearance settings and a native Mac window.

17 October 2026 · v1.5.2-mac

Vanta for Mac now opens in its own window

Vanta 1.5.2 for macOS runs in a native Mac window with normal menus and shortcuts, and never opens your web browser.
