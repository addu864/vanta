Vanta - what is (and isn't) in this download

Included:
- Vanta's own code and UI (vanta/, assets/). No license file has been chosen for Vanta yet; all rights reserved by its author unless stated otherwise.
- Python runtime (LICENSE.txt in the Windows build is Python's PSF license).
- Windows: Microsoft WebView2 SDK loader/WinForms DLLs (redistributable per the WebView2 SDK license).
- skinview3d (MIT, assets/vendor/skinview3d.LICENSE.txt).

Not included (downloaded on first launch from the official sources, on your computer):
- Minecraft client, libraries and assets (Mojang) and the Fabric loader.
- All mods (Modrinth), and the Trailer-look packs: Bare Bones, Fresh Animations,
  Bare Bones x Fresh Animations, Complementary Reimagined and Complementary Unbound (Modrinth).
  These are covered by their authors' own licenses and terms.
- Skins: Skindex skins are downloaded from skindex.pro when you open them and cached
  on your computer; Minecraft's default skins are read from your own Minecraft files.
  Vanta ships only its own original skins.
